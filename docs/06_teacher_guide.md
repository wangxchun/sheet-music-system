# 06 Teacher Guide

How to use the catalogue, on one page. Print it and post it in the studio.

The spreadsheet is in Traditional Chinese. Tab and column names are shown as they appear, with an English gloss.

## Find a piece

1. Open the **Google Sheets** app on your phone and open `鋼琴教材目錄` (piano catalogue).
2. Switch to the **`搜尋`** (search) tab.
3. In **B1**, type part of a title, composer or book title, for example `Arabesque`, `Clementi` or `小奏鳴曲` (sonatina).
4. The **book title, page and location** are listed below.
5. Find the book by its location: `A-2-05` = **cabinet A, shelf 2, fifth book**.

## Choose a piece for a student

1. Switch to the **`曲目`** (pieces) tab.
2. Tap **Data → Filter views** and pick a view, for example `初級曲目` (beginner pieces).
3. Or tap the filter icon next to a column heading and filter by level, period or style yourself.

A filter view only changes your own screen, not what other teachers see.

## Send a student a demo recording

Find the link in the `示範音檔` (demo recording) column on the pieces or search tab, copy it, and send it to the student or parent.

Share only the recording link. **Do not share the whole catalogue.**

## Adding or editing pieces

- **Choose level, period and style from the dropdowns.** Don't type them yourself, or the filters won't find them.
- **Grey columns** (book title, location) fill in automatically; don't type in them.
- The owner handles adding whole books. Please pass new books to the owner.

## Borrowing and returning books

- When you put a book back, return it to the place shown **on its spine code**.
- If a spine label falls off, tell the owner so it can be replaced.

## If something goes wrong

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Search finds nothing | Try another keyword, such as just the composer |
| It's in the catalogue but not on the shelf | It may be on loan or misplaced; tell the owner |
| You changed something by mistake | Tell the owner; it can be restored from the version history |
